Quick rules

  • Select (1): click/drag to select soldiers. Move (2/V): click ground to move. Right-click a soldier to cancel its task.
  • Soldiers move 1 tile/second in rectilinear shortest paths.
  • Riflemen: range 10 (hill 12, mountain 14), fire while moving, 1 shot per 2s.
  • Grenadiers: auto-throw at enemies in range (3s windup, then 2s reload). Won't throw close enough to hurt themselves.
  • MG: range 20, needs 2 nearby soldiers to fire bursts.
  • Dig only adjacent to your trench. MGs build next to trench (not in trench).
  • Lose your officer and you can't issue squad/formation orders until the next recruit (an officer) arrives.
  • Win by eliminating the enemy, or by most kills at 10:00.
